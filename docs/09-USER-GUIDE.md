# 9. Short guide for staff

## Selling (everyone)
1. Go to **Sales → New sale** (the big button), or **New sale** in the menu.
2. Tap product photos to add them, or search or scan a barcode. Tap a line to change the quantity or price.
3. Choose the customer:
   - **Walk-in** is the default (pay in full).
   - Search an existing customer.
   - Use **+ New** to create one on the spot (name and phone).
   - Wholesale and Reseller customers get wholesale prices automatically.
4. Under **Payment**, choose Cash, MTN MoMo or Orange Money, pick the receiving number, and enter the amount. Paying less makes it a **credit sale** with a due date (within the customer's limit). **Reserve** holds the goods for later pickup.
5. Send the **receipt**: WhatsApp, PDF, or a screenshot.

## Payments, returns, cancel
- **Open a sale:**
  - **+ Payment** for deposits and balances.
  - **Return** to choose the items and quantity, a reason, and whether they go back to stock.
  - **Cancel** (managers) needs a reason and refunds by the original method.
- **Customers** shows who owes what, the purchase history, and a WhatsApp/SMS **reminder** button.

## End of day
Open **Cash handover** in the menu. The app shows what you collected per method. Enter what you are handing over and submit. A manager confirms it.

## Buying and shipping (manager / owner)
1. **Purchases → New:** product, store, date, price in USD or EUR, quantity ("pack of N" splits into units), expiry and photo. B-Stock: import the manifest Excel file.
   - Bought for a client? Tick **Client order**. Paying later? Choose **On credit** to create a supplier bill.
2. **Containers → New:** add the purchases waiting in US storage, then move the status along as it ships.
3. Add costs as they happen: freight, trucking, loading, BESC, customs, port, unloading. A car's customs should be set to **apply to that car only**. Set the container's exchange rate if it is not 600.
4. **Receive** at the warehouse: choose the branch and note missing or damaged items. The goods are now on sale, with prices set from landed cost × markup.

## Branches, transfers, stock counts
- **Branch transfers:** pick the from/to branches and the items, then **send**. The receiving branch taps **Receive** and enters what arrived; anything missing is recorded as a loss.
- **Stock count:** choose a branch and category (blind if you like), count, and submit. A manager reviews and **applies** it, and the differences become adjustments.
- **Adjust stock** (theft, damage, expiry, found) always asks for a reason.

## Assets (manager / owner)
Things the business owns and uses but doesn't sell: a stacker or forklift, truck, motorbike, tanks, generator, freezers, shelves, computers, land.
1. **Assets → Register an asset:** add the name, type, photo and serial/plate, then the date, seller, price (USD, EUR or F) and extra costs (shipping, customs, installation). Then say how it was paid; **Not paid yet** creates a bill under Suppliers. Finally, choose the branch and who keeps it.
2. Kargo shows its **value today** and how much it loses each month. That monthly amount is counted in Profit & loss.
3. Open an asset to **Add cost** (repairs, fuel, insurance; this also goes into Expenses), **Move / give to** another branch or person, or **Sell / write off** (sold, broken, stolen, given away). A reason is always required.
4. Service, insurance and papers dates warn you on Home 30 days before. Download the register with **Excel**.

## Reports
11 tabs: overview, day by day, items (best sellers, most profit), timing (best day of week and time of month), people (by staff), inventory (fast/slow, dead stock), forecast, containers (landed cost and profit per container), monthly P&L (including asset depreciation), expiry, losses. Filter by dates and branch, and download Excel.

## Team & safety
- **Team & access:** add each staff member with their own login (email or phone) and password, choose their role and branch, and switch individual permissions on or off. **Roles** lets you edit or create roles.
- **Activity log:** see who did what, where, when and why, with filters and an Excel export.
- **Settings → Backup:** download everything (including photos) regularly. **Restore** brings it back.
- **No internet?** Keep selling. An "N to sync" badge shows until the connection returns, then everything sends automatically.
